# Self-scheduled round robin without fixtures

Each League defines a format (single or double round-robin) but does not generate a fixed fixture list or play order. Players choose when to play and pick opponents from their Remaining opponents list on the Kiosk. We chose this because office fun leagues are informal — people play when the table is free, not on a calendar. The alternative (pre-generated schedules) adds admin overhead and conflicts with a single shared table with no booking system.
